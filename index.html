Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026.

This page describes the privacy practices of "rclone", a personal OAuth client created by Nguyen Manh Quy for his own use.

Data accessed

With the owner's consent, the client accesses the owner's own Google Drive files (including document metadata) through the Google Drive API, solely to let the owner back up and manage his own files with rclone.

Data sharing

Data accessed through this client is not shared with, sold to, or processed for any third party, and is not used for advertising.
